The engine plays at full strength. Pawns promote to queens automatically. Tap a piece, then tap its destination.

Openings — guided lines

Play the book move each turn; the other side answers from theory. When the line ends, keep playing against the full-strength engine, then analyze.

Middlegame — random positions

Generates a fresh, sound middlegame (both sides play sensible but varied moves), drops you in, and you play the full-strength engine. Goal: build a winning advantage (+3.0), then convert.

Endgames — technique

Classic must-know endings. Play to checkmate or a draw against perfect resistance.
